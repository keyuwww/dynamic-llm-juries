Accept rate = share ending in VERDICT: CORRECT. 'Broke' = looped without finishing (8,192-token cap) or gave no verdict; it counts as not accepting. Changes are paired against 'none' on the same items; 95% intervals resample whole questions. Push sizes are per layer, in units of the belief projection's spread at that layer; every direction gets the same push length. 'x*lam' rows SCALE instead: each item's own position along the direction (around its average) is multiplied by lam (0 = removed, -5 = reversed and stretched, 5 = stretched), so the push differs by item; a random direction varies less across items, so its scaled push is smaller.
