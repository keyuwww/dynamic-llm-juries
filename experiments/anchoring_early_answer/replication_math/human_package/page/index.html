How to label (read once)

Each item is a competition math problem from DAPO-Math-17k (integer answer) and two AI responses. The benchmark's answer key says one response is right and the other is wrong. Your job is to check the response the key marks wrong: is its final integer really wrong? Please label all 66 items.

  • Clearly wrong — its final integer is wrong and the key's integer is right.
  • Actually correct — its final integer is right: the key is wrong.
  • Ambiguous — the problem is unclear or admits more than one defensible answer.

Write a one-line reason (a short derivation where you side with the response). Use paper, a calculator or code if you need to, but do not ask an AI assistant: these labels are the paper's human check. Labels save automatically when you press Save; you can come back and change them. Keys: 1/2/3 pick a label, Ctrl+Enter saves and moves on.
