Should a judge answer the question before grading it?

8 LLM judges · 808 human-labelled BFF responses · 3 reference conditions

  1. Grading against your own answer doesn't help on average (-0.2 points over 8 judges).It helps three thinking-off judges (Nemotron-Super +4.6, Qwen3.5-9B +2.9, DeepSeek +2.5) and hurts gpt-oss-20b (−5.3) and Nemotron-Nano (−3.3).
  2. When a judge's own answer is wrong, the reasoning judges follow it: -8.6 and -16.3 points.gpt-oss-120b and gpt-oss-20b gain little when their answer is right. Some thinking-off judges still gain when theirs is wrong, so writing an answer first may just make them read more carefully.
  3. A correct reference is what matters: the expert answer lifts every judge, by +8.2 points on average.Nemotron-Super goes from 71.3% to 87.5%, past gpt-oss-120b without a reference (80.2%).

What this means for the router
  • Don't ask the judge to answer first by default. Give it a reference only when that reference is likely right.
  • The disagreement trigger already flags items where judges are unsure; those are the ones worth a strong model's answer or an expert check.

10/19 runs: one sample per judge at temperature 0.7, max 4,096 tokens, 2-of-3 human labels. Own answers come from the same model with thinking off where that can be switched off. Right / wrong labels for those answers are LLM grades against the expert reference (the two graders agree on 82%).
