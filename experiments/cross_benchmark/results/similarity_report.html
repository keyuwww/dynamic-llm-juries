Where the 8 judges agree, where their errors overlap, and how much of the gap to "any judge right" a judge router or a jury could actually recover.

How much can choosing or combining judges recover?

Averaged over 9 benchmarks: the best single judge is right 83.5% of the time and "any judge right" 96.7%, a gap of 13.3 points. Only 5.4 points of it are errors a majority of the other judges gets right; 7.8 points are right only for a minority, and 3.3 points for no judge.

A jury that only sees the 8 verdicts can reach at most 88.6% even in-sample (+5.1 points over the best judge), and 82.5% cross-validated. Everything above that sits on items where most judges are wrong: a router can only win it with item-level information that tells it which minority judge to trust, and on single-judge rescues that is mostly a lucky guess on a yes/no verdict.
