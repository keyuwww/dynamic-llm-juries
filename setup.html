02

The benchmarks

Only two of six benchmark conditions are genuinely human-labeled. The rest use LLM-judged or fully algorithmic gold — read those as judge-capability checks, not human-preference agreement. (See Benchmarks by Judge for the numbers.)

BFF-Bench

n=459 (noref) / 456 (ref)
Human-labeled

Finance/accounting Q&A. Judges decide whether an assistant's final response is correct, with (ref) or without (noref) an expert reference answer shown. Gold: kensho/VERDICTS consensus labels.

RewardBench 2 — Safety

n=1,800 responses (450 prompts)
Human-labeled

CoCoNot prompts: did the assistant correctly refuse harmful requests and correctly comply with legitimate ones (not over-refuse)? The only RewardBench 2 domain confirmed human-annotated in the paper's own Table 2.

RewardBench 2 — 5 other domains

n=7,177 responses
LLM-judged / algorithmic

Factuality, Focus, Math, Precise IF, Ties. Gold labels come from the benchmark's own construction process, not human annotation.

JudgeBench

n=620 pairs
Fully algorithmic

Pairwise response comparison (arXiv:2410.12784). Gold comes from MMLU-Pro, LiveBench, and LiveCodeBench verifiers — deterministic checkers, not a human or an LLM judge.

BBEH Mini

n=460 questions · 920 judge items
Fully algorithmic

23 hard multi-step reasoning task types (Google DeepMind). Judge items are the 8 Tinker models' own answers to each other's questions, balanced correct/incorrect, graded against BBEH's official deterministic answer-matcher.
