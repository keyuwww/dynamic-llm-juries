About 45 seconds. Quick recap of the measure: before seeing any candidate, the judge answers the question seven times on its own. Its belief in a candidate is how many of those seven answers reach the candidate’s final answer, from 0/7 to 7/7. The judge never grades its own text, so this is not self-preference. Last week, three Qwen judges approved 18% of wrong answers they never reach and 74% of those they always reach, using the labels as the benchmarks released them. This week I asked four questions; each card points to the slides that answer it. Up front: a few of last week’s numbers changed, mostly because some “wrong” labels turned out to be disputed.
