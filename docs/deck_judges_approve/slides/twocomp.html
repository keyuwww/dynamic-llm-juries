4.8 · INSIDE THE MODEL

Correlated or not? Two different comparisons

COMPARISON 1 · ACROSS ROLES

“Right” while solving vs “right” while judging

≈ 0

Not correlated, even at a matched spot. The verdict does not reuse the solver’s sense of “correct”.

COMPARISON 2 · INSIDE THE JUDGE

“I’d give this answer” vs “this is right”

0.72 – 0.93

Strongly correlated. At the verdict, agreeing with it reads as it being right.

So the tension resolves: the judge doesn’t borrow the solver’s signal. It re-solves, checks for a match, and treats a match as “correct”. Solving and judging are linked through the answer, not a shared sense of rightness.

Comparison 1 numbers: cosine 0.00 to 0.29 for the neutral FINAL: role vs the judge, Qwen-9B and Qwen-35B, RB2 Math and BBEH. Comparison 2: belief direction vs the judge's correctness direction, 0.72 to 0.93 on BFF and 0.77 on BBEH. Supporting behaviour: with thinking on, approvals of wrong answers come with a re-solve landing on that answer (86 to 96 percent); with its own answer committed first, approval almost entirely tracks whether its answer matches.
