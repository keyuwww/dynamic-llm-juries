3.8 · INSIDE THE MODEL · TAKEAWAY

Correlated or not? Two different comparisons

COMPARISON 1 · ACROSS ROLES

“Right” while solving vs “right” while judging

0.0 – 0.3

Not correlated, even at a matched spot, in both Qwen models. The verdict does not reuse the solver’s sense of “correct”.

COMPARISON 2 · INSIDE ONE ROLE

“I’d give this answer” vs “this is right”

0.5 – 0.8

Correlated at the verdict, less when writing. Part of it is the pull toward saying CORRECT.

So: the judge doesn’t borrow the solver’s signal. It re-solves, checks for a match, and leans toward “correct” when the answer matches. Solving and judging connect through the answer, not a shared sense of rightness.

Comparison 1 is settled for Qwen3.5-9B and Qwen3.6-35B (matched-position test). Comparison 2 is likely: belief and correctness directions built on separate questions, 0.49 to 0.80 at the judge vs 0.02 to 0.51 when writing; on BFF much of it runs through the CORRECT-vs-INCORRECT lean. The behavioural evidence (transcripts, commit-first) carries the mechanism more firmly than the directions do.
