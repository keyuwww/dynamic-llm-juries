Thinking off hides it: in writing, most approvals (67–86%) only check the given steps.

About 1 minute. GLM-5.3-Flash (a different model family from every judge) labelled 7,443 transcripts (4,254 thinking off, 3,189 thinking on; Qwens, RB2 Math and BFF); 8 labels hand-checked. Ranges are over the four belief levels, three Qwens pooled. Both rows are out of all approved wrong answers, so row 2 is part of row 1 (with thinking on, almost every re-solve lands on the candidate’s answer). With thinking on the belief staircase is steeper: 3, 24, 50, 79 percent. Rejections also come with a re-solve, one that lands on a different answer. Backup: 4.1 to 4.3.
