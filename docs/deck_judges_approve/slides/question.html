1.1 · INTRODUCTION · THE QUESTION

Question and hypothesis

OUR HYPOTHESIS

A judge approves wrong answers it would give itself, whoever wrote them.

Not “self-preference”: the judge never wrote any candidate.

QUESTION 1

Does approval rise with how strongly the judge believes the answer?

QUESTION 2

What inside the model links its own answer to its verdict?

Related work: Panickssery 2024 (self-preference), Roytburg 2026 (much self-preference is judge quality), Goel 2025 (judges favour models with similar mistakes), Krumdick 2025, No Free Labels (judges agree with experts mainly where they can answer themselves; source of BFF-Bench), Lin 2025 (solving and judging weakly linked; self-reference strengthens the link).
