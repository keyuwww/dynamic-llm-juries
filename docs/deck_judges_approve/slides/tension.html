4.1 · INSIDE THE MODEL

The tension: behaviour vs the model’s insides

BEHAVIOUR

The verdict follows what the judge would answer itself.

INSIDE THE MODEL

Its “right vs wrong” direction while writing looked unrelated to the one at the verdict.

cosine ≈ 0

BUT A CONFOUND

The two were read at different spots in the text.

end of answer vs after “VERDICT:”

Direction = average hidden state of right answers minus wrong answers, within each question. Cosine 1 = same, 0 = unrelated.

So we needed to know whether "solving and judging encode correctness differently" was real, or only an artifact of where we read the hidden state.
