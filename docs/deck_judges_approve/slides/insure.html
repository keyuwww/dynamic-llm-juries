Both arrows partly just mean “about to say CORRECT”.

LIMITS

Qwen only: no second family inside

Side finding (appendix A.3): the judge’s correctness arrow is unrelated to the solver’s (cosine 0.0–0.3). The judge doesn’t reuse a ready-made “this is right” signal; it re-solves and checks for a match (§4).

The lean is the model's own CORRECT-vs-INCORRECT verdict direction; we project it out of both arrows and recompute the cosine (full data, best layer): Qwen-9B BFF 0.93 to 0.86, Qwen-9B BBEH 0.77 to 0.77, Qwen-35B BBEH 0.77 to 0.76, Qwen-35B BFF 0.72 to 0.23. Details for every role and the solving-vs-judging test are in appendix A.2 to A.5.
