5.3 · INTERVENTIONS · METHOD

Two causal tests

1 · DOES SEEING THE ANSWER PULL THE SOLVE?

Show the candidate, but ask the model to solve it, not judge.

Read: how often its answer becomes the candidate’s, by belief.

If pull exists: well above 0% even at belief 0/7.

2 · COMMIT FIRST, THEN JUDGE

The model’s own earlier answer sits in the chat before it sees the candidate.

Read: approval of wrong answers, by belief.

If the candidate steers it: 0/7 approvals drop.

Three Qwens, thinking off, RB2 Math + BFF, every candidate · about 3,100 runs each on Tinker · GLM matches answers for test 1.

Results on the next two slides. The earlier answer in test 2 is the model's first solve, generated without the candidate and separate from the 7 belief tries.
