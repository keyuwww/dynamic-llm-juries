2.4 · METHOD

Measuring belief, step by step

1 · SOLVE ALONE, 7 TIMES

Only the question. Temperature 0.7, so tries can differ. The candidate is never shown.

2 · COMPARE

GLM-5.3-Flash: does each try reach the candidate’s final answer? JudgeBench letters: exact match.

3 · BELIEF = COUNT

0/7 never reaches it · 3/7 torn · 7/7 always.

Why 7, not 1?

One own answer is a noisy yes/no. Seven give a graded score.

Why GLM to match?

A different family from every judge, so no judge’s family decides what counts as “same”.

Why separate calls?

The judge never sees its own tries while grading.

Example: question "3x + 7 = 31". The candidate says 9. The judge's 7 tries give 8, 8, 9, 8, 8, 8, 8, so belief in 9 is 1/7. The earlier one-sample "matches my answer" label used a single extra try, independent of these 7.
