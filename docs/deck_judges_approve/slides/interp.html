4.2 · INSIDE THE MODEL

Looking inside: the tools

HIDDEN STATE

The model’s internal vector at one token, one per layer. We save it at a chosen token, every 2nd layer.

DIRECTION

Average state of right answers minus wrong answers. Each question’s average is subtracted first, so “hard question” can’t leak in.

COSINE + CEILING

Compare two directions: 1 = same, 0 = unrelated. Ceiling: the same direction from two halves of the questions, the most noise allows.

PROBE TRANSFER

Train a reader in one setting, test it in another, whole questions held out. Catches a shared signal a low cosine can hide.

The difference-of-means direction is the standard method (Marks and Tegmark 2023; Arditi et al. 2024; Rimsky et al. 2024). Two random directions in this space have a cosine of about 0.016. Probes are logistic regressions scored by within-question AUROC (0.5 = chance).
