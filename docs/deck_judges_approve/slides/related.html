1.2 · INTRODUCTION · PRIOR WORK

What is already known, and how we differ

PANICKSSERY 2024

Judges rate their own outputs higher (“self-preference”).

ROYTBURG 2026 (ICML)

Much of that is the judge simply being wrong on the question, not vanity.

GOEL 2025

Judges favour models that make the same mistakes as they do.

LIN 2025 (EMNLP)

Giving the judge its own answer helps only when that answer is right.

WHAT WE ADD

No authorship. The judge never wrote any candidate.

Belief per answer. Measured by re-solving, not one sample.

Inside the model. Hidden states and transcripts show the mechanism.

Our setup directly tests Roytburg's alternative: if the judge favours answers it would give itself, whoever wrote them, then "self-preference" is largely belief. Goel's effect is the model-level version of ours; we measure it per answer.
