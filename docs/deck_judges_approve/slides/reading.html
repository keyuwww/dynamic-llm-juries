Current reading

A JUDGE MOSTLY ASKS

“Does this match what I get?”

It re-derives the answer, out loud when thinking, silently when not. Belief predicts where that lands, so it predicts the verdict.

LOW BELIEF

Errors come from the grading frame. Committing its own answer first removes them: +5 balanced accuracy.

HIGH BELIEF

Errors are shared across families (~80%). Neither prompts nor a second Qwen-size juror fix them.

For a jury: confidently wrong items need a reference outside these models (an expert answer, a much stronger model, a tool or a calculation), not another juror of similar strength. That is reasoning from the juror test, not yet tested with a stronger model.
