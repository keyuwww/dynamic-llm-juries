Settled for four judges in two families. Bigger Qwens are stricter on answers they don't believe (27% to 8% at 0/7) but just as trusting of answers they do believe. Belief is usually right (94% of 7/7 answers are correct), so the damage is the confident-wrong tail. Reasoning amplifies the effect: +35 before reasoning, +54 at the verdict.
