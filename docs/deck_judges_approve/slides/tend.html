TALK · TAKEAWAYS

Takeaways

1. Judges approve what they believe: wrong answers approved 18% → 74% as belief rises (4 judges, 2 families)

2. They check for a match, not for truth: they re-solve and agree when they land on the same answer

3. Low-belief errors are fixable: commit its own answer first, 18% → 1%, +5 balanced accuracy

4. High-belief errors need an outside reference: a second judge shares them; an expert answer cuts them 62% → 12% (BFF)

Next: can a strong model’s answer replace the expert, as a free reference for the items the judge believes?
