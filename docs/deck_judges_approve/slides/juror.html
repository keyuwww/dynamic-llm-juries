A second juror from another family doesn’t fix it

WHAT WE TRIED (EXISTING DATA)

When a Qwen believes an answer (≥ 4/7), hand the verdict to Nemotron-3-Super (NVIDIA), which judged the same items.

77–81%

of the wrong answers a Qwen confidently believes, Nemotron believes too.

Confident wrong beliefs are shared across these families, and a disagreeing juror rejects only about half of them.

Main set, verdicts at C, same items in every column; 95% intervals in out/rigor_checks.md. Believed wrong answers rejected: e.g. Qwen-9B 32% alone, 22% after handing to Nemotron. When Nemotron's own belief is 3/7 or less (13 to 21 items per Qwen), it rejects 46 to 52%, no better than the Qwen. This undercuts the "pick jurors whose beliefs differ" idea for these models: such jurors are rare and not more accurate. A stronger reference (expert answer or a much stronger model) is the likelier fix.
