5.1 · INTERVENTIONS · METHOD

A free test: tell the judge not to re-solve

THE PROMPT (FROM THE NOV 8–9 STUDY)

“Do NOT solve the question yourself from scratch. Verify the assistant’s work instead.”

It was run on the same 459 BFF items, 3 samples each, for the three Qwens and Nemotron.

HOW

Attach our belief scores to those old runs. No new generation.

WHY

If belief works only by re-solving, forbidding it should flatten the staircase.

This prompt was first reported as "doesn't help" (+1 on BFF questions the judge can't solve). Looked at by belief, it tells us something different.
