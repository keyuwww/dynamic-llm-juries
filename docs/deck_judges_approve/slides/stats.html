2.8 · METHOD

How we count an effect

WITHIN EACH JUDGE

Compare belief groups inside one judge, so a lenient judge doesn’t inflate the gap (judge fixed effects).

BELIEF VS ONE SAMPLE

Put both measures in one regression, to see which one carries the effect.

SAME ANSWER, DIFFERENT JUDGES

One wrong answer, graded by three Qwens that believe it differently. The text is fixed, so answer quality can’t explain the gap.

UNCERTAINTY

95% intervals by resampling whole questions, since answers to one question aren’t independent.

The headline "+54" is the 7/7 minus 0/7 difference in approval of wrong answers, from a regression with judge fixed effects. The same-answer comparison adds candidate fixed effects and works only where the judges disagree in belief.
